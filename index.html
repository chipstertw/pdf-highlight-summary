How it works

Organised like the book

Chapters and sections come from the PDF's bookmarks. Smaller headings come from the type on the page, including bold headings that start a paragraph. Each highlight sits under the headings it was found beneath.

Highlights, cleaned up

The text under each highlight is read from the page and line-break hyphens are repaired. A sentence you highlighted across a page break becomes one entry, and a highlighted heading is not repeated. Notes appear under their highlight.

Private and repeatable

Everything runs in this browser tab, and the PDF is never uploaded. There is no AI involved, so the same PDF always gives the same summary. The only network request is the optional DOI/ISBN lookup, and it sends nothing but the number.

Works with highlights, underlines and strike-throughs saved in the PDF by Preview, and by most other apps that use standard PDF annotations. Scanned PDFs need text recognition (OCR) first.
